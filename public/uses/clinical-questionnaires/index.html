Structured clinical-style forms

Svelp helps structure clinical-style questionnaires — study instruments with consent blocks, signature lines, Likert scales, and symptom checklists — and removes manual data entry from the paper loop.

What Svelp provides

  • Consent blocks with introduction, purpose, confidentiality, and acknowledgement
  • Participant and researcher signature lines with printed-name and date fields
  • Structured instruments: checklists, scales, and matrices
  • Local-only storage — records stay on the device unless explicitly exported
  • Deterministic scanning with conservative review routing

What Svelp is not

Svelp is a questionnaire and form workflow tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or clinical decision support, is not HIPAA or GDPR certification, and makes no regulatory claims. Compliance obligations for your study remain yours.
