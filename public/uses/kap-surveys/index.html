Open Svelp

KAP surveys on paper, scanned in batches

Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice studies mix question formats: factual yes/no/don't-know items, attitude Likert batteries, and practice grids. Svelp structures each section with the right question type and prints them scanner-ready.

KAP building blocks

  • Knowledge: single choice and yes/no with custom "don't know" coding
  • Attitude: Likert batteries sharing one reusable scale
  • Practice: frequency matrices over a shared scale
  • Consent blocks with purpose and confidentiality sections
  • Compact print profile keeps long KAP instruments short on paper
  • Batch scan completed forms and review uncertain answers

Custom yes/no coding

yesno knows_danger "Can unboiled water be unsafe?"
  yes 1
  no 0
  required

Scale sharing

Define an agreement or frequency scale once; every attitude statement and practice row reuses it, keeping codes consistent for analysis.
