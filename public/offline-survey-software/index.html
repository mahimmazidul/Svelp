Open Svelp

Offline-first survey software

Svelp is built for places with unreliable connectivity. Install it once as a PWA and everything — building, printing, scanning, review, export — keeps working offline on your device.

What offline means in Svelp

  • Questionnaires, scans, and responses are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB)
  • Scanning and recognition run on your device
  • The app installs as a PWA and opens without a network
  • No account, sign-up, or subscription exists
  • Move projects between devices explicitly with MAHIM portability files
  • Export research data as CSV or JSON whenever you are ready

No backend involved

Normal Svelp use requires no server. There is no cloud sync to trust, and nothing to configure. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly export or share a file.

Practical limits

Local browser storage is subject to your browser and device quotas and your device security. Browser data can be cleared by resetting the browser; use the built-in export and MAHIM transfer tools for backups. Svelp makes no absolute security guarantees beyond keeping data local.

Install and work offline

Open Svelp in a modern browser and install it as an app from the address bar.
