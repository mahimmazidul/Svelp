About Svelp

Svelp — Survey Help, simplified. — exists because a lot of important research still happens on paper, in places where connectivity and hardware are unreliable, and manual data entry wastes weeks.

Why scanner-generated forms

General-purpose form recognition is unreliable. Svelp prints its own forms with alignment markers and a QR identity on every page, so recognition measures known geometry instead of guessing at unknown layouts.

Why deterministic recognition

Mark detection in Svelp is geometry and measurement — the same scan always produces the same result. When confidence is low, Svelp says "review this" instead of guessing. There is no AI model interpreting your data.

Why local-first

Research data is sensitive and connectivity is not guaranteed. Svelp keeps data on the device that collected it and moves data only through files you explicitly create and share.

Status

Svelp is actively developed. The scanner calibration framework is complete and validated in simulation; physical validation with user-supplied printed samples is pending.
