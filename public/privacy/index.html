Privacy at Svelp

Svelp is local-first by architecture, not by policy promise: the application has no backend, so questionnaire and response data stay in your browser storage during normal use.

Where data lives

  • Projects, questionnaires, scans, and responses are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB)
  • Scanning, recognition, and PDF generation run on your device
  • The installed PWA works offline from local caches
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking scripts are included

What Svelp does not do

  • No account or sign-up exists
  • No cloud sync — there is no server to receive your data
  • No background transmission of questionnaire or response content

Moving data deliberately

You choose when data leaves a device: CSV/JSON exports, print PDFs, and MAHIM portability files are all explicit actions you take.

Limits of local storage

Browser storage is subject to device quotas and to your browser profile: clearing site data deletes local projects, so use the built-in export tools for backups. Device security (encryption, screen lock) is provided by your operating system, not by Svelp.
