Open Svelp

From paper to structured data

Svelp turns completed paper questionnaires into structured data on your device. Photograph stacks of forms with any phone, import images or PDFs in batches, and let deterministic, geometry-based recognition read the marks — flagging anything uncertain for your review.

How scanning works

  1. IdentifyEach printed page carries a QR code identifying the questionnaire, version, respondent, and page number — pages can arrive in any order.
  2. NormalizeAlignment markers drive local perspective correction, rotation handling, and lighting normalization.
  3. Read marksRecognition measures filled bubbles and grids geometrically. It is deterministic: the same image always produces the same result.
  4. ReviewLow-confidence marks, double marks, crossed-out answers, and unreadable regions go to a review queue — Svelp prefers "uncertain" over silently wrong.
  5. ExportAccepted answers flow into the response table and out to CSV or JSON with codebooks.

Language independence

Mark recognition reads geometry, not words — Bangla, English, or any language questionnaire scans identically. Free-text handwriting is never read automatically; text fields stay available for manual entry.

Honest scope

Svelp does not reliably interpret arbitrary unrelated paper forms. It works with forms generated by Svelp, or forms matching the same page geometry. Physical calibration with real printed samples is still pending, and very faint or rough marks may route to review rather than auto-accept.
